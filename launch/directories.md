Name: Wordroot

One-liner: Look up a word and follow it back to its root.

Description: A dictionary that shows where words come from. Clean definitions plus the full etymology chain, Old English to Latin to Proto-Indo-European. Free on the web, $0.99 upfront on iPhone and Mac.

Category: Education / Reference / Dictionary
