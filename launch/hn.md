Show HN: Wordroot – Look up a word and follow it back to its root

Dictionaries stop at the definition. The interesting part is the chain behind it. Wordroot looks up any English word and walks it back: Middle English, Old English, Latin, Greek, all the way to Proto-Indo-European where it is known. It is the tool I wanted every time I fell into a Wiktionary hole. Web at wordroot.heyitsmejosh.com, source on GitHub.
