Privacy Policy

Etyma (App Store name: Wordroot) · Last updated 11 August 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The app and website have no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, and nothing you type is sent to us.

Word lookups

When you look up a word, the app requests that word's page directly from the public Wiktionary API operated by the Wikimedia Foundation. That request is not routed through any server we control. Wikimedia's handling of such requests is covered by their privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Only your search text while the app is open. It is not persisted after you close the app.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
